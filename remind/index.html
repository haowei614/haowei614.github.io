ReMIND

AI-powered requirements conflict resolution through multi-agent dialectical negotiation. Built on research from QUARE and ArgRE.

Based on RE 2026 & IEEE ACCESS MVP — Early Preview

What is ReMIND?

ReMIND (Requirements conflict resolution through Multi-agent Intelligent Negotiation and Dialectics) is a tool that helps software teams detect and resolve conflicting requirements before they become costly downstream problems.

It uses multiple AI agents — each representing a different quality concern (safety, efficiency, trustworthiness, etc.) — to systematically surface conflicts and negotiate balanced solutions through structured argumentation.

MA

Multi-Agent Negotiation

Quality-specialized agents debate requirements from different stakeholder perspectives.

CR

Conflict Detection

Automatically surfaces cross-quality conflicts that are hard to catch manually.

FA

Formal Argumentation

Resolves disputes through structured dialectical protocols, not implicit aggregation.

Why ReMIND?

Most AI-based requirements tools stop at summarization or classification. ReMIND goes further — it resolves conflicts with traceable reasoning.
